The shipped light picture on a Dark Mode desktop: the title bar follows the system, the picture reads as a lit sheet.
Why the light artwork ships
–A DMG background is one fixed picture — it can’t switch with the system appearance.
–With a background picture set, Finder keeps the icon area in its light appearance: file names stay black.
–So ship the light artwork. It keeps the labels at 19:1 and works on both desktops; the dark one fails the labels.
–Re-check on the macOS 26 release build before shipping — this is long-standing Finder behaviour, not a documented API.
