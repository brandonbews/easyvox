og:image in a generic chat link preview (~29%).
Recommendation
–GitHub takes one social preview image and og:image can’t follow the reader’s theme either, so each ships one version.
–Default to dark for both. Most link previews sit on white with no border; the dark card keeps its edge there, and the wave reads at 5.6:1 (light card: 3.7:1).
–Keep the light pair for the press kit and light pages. Export PNG under 1 MB; set og:image:width and og:image:height.
