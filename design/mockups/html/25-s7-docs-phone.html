Permissions

EasyVox needs two permissions: Microphone, so it can hear you, and Accessibility, so it can paste into the app you’re using. macOS asks for each one the first time it’s needed.

Everything stays on this Mac

EasyVox only listens while you hold ⌥ Space. Your speech is turned into text by the model inside the app, and the audio never leaves your Mac.

Microphone

The first time you hold ⌥ Space, macOS asks whether EasyVox can use the microphone.

1
Hold ⌥ Space in any app.
2
When macOS asks, click Allow.
3
Keep holding and say a few words. They appear in the pill.

Accessibility

Accessibility lets EasyVox paste into other apps. Without it, the pill still shows your words, but they can’t be pasted at your cursor.

1
Open System Settings and go to Privacy & Security › Accessibility.
2
Turn on EasyVox. You may be asked for your password.
3
Go back to any app, hold ⌥ Space and talk. When you let go, the text is pasted at your cursor.
continued

Reset permissions

If EasyVox stops hearing you or can’t paste — for example after you move the app — reset its permissions and allow them again. In Terminal, run:

TerminalCopy
tccutil reset Microphone app.easyvox
tccutil reset Accessibility app.easyvox

Then quit EasyVox from the menu bar, open it again and follow the steps above. Still stuck? See Troubleshooting.
