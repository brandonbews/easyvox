Esc to cancel

How it works

Nothing to set up after the first run.

⌥option
Step 1
Hold ⌥Space

In any app, wherever your cursor is. A small pill appears at the bottom of the screen.

Can we push lunch to one?
Step 2
Talk

Your words show up in the pill as you say them, so you know it’s listening.

Messages
Can we push lunch to one?
Step 3
Let go — it’s pasted

EasyVox pastes the text at your cursor. Press Esc before you let go to cancel.

Out of the way until you need it.

Runs on your Mac

The speech model ships inside the app. Your audio never leaves your Mac.

Works in any app

Mail, Messages, Notes, Slack, Xcode — if it takes text, EasyVox can type into it.

See your words as you speak

The pill shows the transcript live, so you know it heard you before you let go.

History stays on this Mac

Find something you said earlier. Keep it, limit it, or turn it off.

Keyboard-first

One shortcut to talk and Esc to cancel. Change ⌥Space to any shortcut you like.

Free and open source

MIT licensed. Read the code, build it yourself, or help make it better.
