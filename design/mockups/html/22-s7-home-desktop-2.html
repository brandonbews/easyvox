What you say stays on this Mac.

The speech model is part of the app, so your voice is turned into text right here — never sent anywhere to be transcribed. And because EasyVox is open source, you don’t have to take our word for it.

How permissions work
No account and no sign-in

Download it, allow two permissions, and start talking.

No internet needed to dictate

Turn Wi‑Fi off and EasyVox works exactly the same.

History on this Mac only

Choose how long it’s kept, or turn it off.

Questions

More answers in the docs, or ask on GitHub.

Is it really free?

Yes. EasyVox is free and open source under the MIT licence. There’s no paid tier, no trial and no account.

Does it need the internet?

No. The speech model ships inside the app, so you can dictate with Wi‑Fi turned off. Your audio never leaves your Mac.

Which languages?

EasyVox 1.0 is tuned for English. The docs list every language the built-in model supports and how well each one works.

Which Macs?

Any Mac running macOS 14 or later. Transcription is fastest on Macs with Apple silicon.

How do I uninstall?

Quit EasyVox from the menu bar and drag it from Applications to the Bin. To remove your history too, clear it in Settings › History first.
