## Description

Onboarding opens at launch whenever a required step is missing, and there's no stored "done" flag (DESIGN.md, Onboarding). So someone who keeps Accessibility off on purpose, and pastes from the clipboard themselves, sees the setup window at every launch, and closes it with "Finish later".

Options: keep it as is, or add a "Don't ask again" for Accessibility. That would be the one reason to store a flag.

Recommendation: keep it as is until someone finds it annoying. Built in d989050.
