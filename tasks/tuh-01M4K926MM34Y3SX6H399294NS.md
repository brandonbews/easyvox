## Description

Brandon's check at an unlocked screen; agents never take it. Built in f424519.

Setup: `scripts/bundle.sh && open dist/EasyVox.app`, and grant Accessibility and Microphone again after a rebuild (the ad-hoc signature changes).

Check:
1. Hold the shortcut, say nothing for 2-3 s, release. Expect: nothing pasted, clipboard unchanged, no History entry, and the overlay briefly says "Didn't catch anything" before hiding. Is the note readable and long enough on screen?
2. Same with a little room noise (typing, a chair). Expect the same.
3. Hold, say one soft word ("OK") at your quietest normal voice, release. Expect it pasted.
4. Start talking right after pressing. Do the first words show up in about half a second?

Brandon, 11 Oct, hands-on round on b31d2c4 (permissions reset, fresh install), walked through step by step: holding the shortcut in silence showed 'Didn't catch that' for 4 s, which he said feels right; dictation felt good.
